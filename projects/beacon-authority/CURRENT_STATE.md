| Generations on chain (ba5) | **Yes** | Sync follows admin-signed `T_BEACON_CERT` / `T_CONFIG` along the blocks to verify a tip of a later generation; older generations are superseded; the beacon signs with `keys/beacon-signing.next` once certified (rotation, `--suite rotation`), and stands down when no key it holds is certified; miners prefer the highest generation |
| Checkpoint start (ba5b) | **Yes** | A checkpoint is an epoch start opening with the genesis renewal; every epoch's first block carries its committee; a miner starts at the previous checkpoint, warms up exactly (every effect on held accounts), validates Full from the current one, and seals once its history covers the transaction validity window ([B015](DECISIONS.md#b015--a-checkpoint-is-where-a-miner-can-start-an-epoch-start-self-describing), [B016](DECISIONS.md#b016--warm-up-is-exact-or-it-stops)); renewals and rotation read slot times. A fresh node commits nothing before the tip verifies — normative in [BLOCK_PIPELINE.md](../../docs/architecture/BLOCK_PIPELINE.md#checkpoints-and-late-join), [WIRE_SCHEMA.md](../../docs/contracts/WIRE_SCHEMA.md) |
| Fresh node's trust root | **Yes, when pinned** | A miner pinning genesis checks its starting checkpoint against `AUTHORITY_PROOF` (replayed from genesis, admin rotations and certificates included); a relay can pin genesis. Unpinned nodes take their first block on trust |
| Certificates out of band (ba5c) | **Yes** | Every node keeps the highest certificate it sees (`STATUS` `certificate`, `TX_ADD`, an operator's `submit-tx`), persisted in `data/beacon-cert.dat`; until a block commits it, older generations' tips above its cutover are refused, a beacon holding no key it names stands down, one holding it commits nothing past the cutover but a certificate. On chain a certificate commits by the block after its cutover; the cutover in force is the block before the commit; no cutover = none ([B017](DECISIONS.md#b017--certificates-out-of-band-bound-the-old-generation-the-cutover-bounds-the-commit)) — normative in [BEACON_AUTHORITY.md § Certificates out of band](../../docs/contracts/BEACON_AUTHORITY.md#certificates-out-of-band) |
| Design, part 2 (promotion) | **Decided (bp0)** | [DESIGN.md § Part 2](DESIGN.md#part-2--promotion): a rewind is a restart; every node adopts a cutover on its own; certificate v2 (`cutoverHash`, gateway `dialHints`), a promoted relay as the new beacon, no liveness charges across the outage, host clock with a skew bound, pool / registry rebuilt (B022–B024). bp1 done |
| Rewind (bp1) | **Yes** | A held certificate whose cutover is below a node's tip orders a rewind: the node writes `data/rewind.dat`, restarts in-process (a fresh server in `main()`), cuts its ledger after the cutover (or joins fresh) before mounting it, and reports it on `STATUS.rewound` — unattended, on beacon, relays and miners (B022, B023), within seven days of slots, deeper on the operator's `--approve-rewind` (B025); a node back online rewinds too — normative in [BEACON_AUTHORITY.md § Rewind](../../docs/contracts/BEACON_AUTHORITY.md#rewind). |
| Certificate v2 (bp2a) | **Yes** | A cutover comes with its block's hash (`cutoverHash`) and commits right after it; it may not drop the block that committed the generation in force; a node holding another block at the cutover halts instead of rewinding; a node behind the cutover syncs to the vouched block with no tip; a node with no blocks is bounded by its upstream's `STATUS` certificate; `mk-beacon-cert --cutover` reads the block from a node and shows the depth; `dialHints` carried (used in bp2b) (B026) — normative in [BEACON_AUTHORITY.md](../../docs/contracts/BEACON_AUTHORITY.md#later-generations--t_beacon_cert-record-18). Next: bp2b (certificates pushed up, relay candidate lists, re-ranking) |
| Equivocation halt (ba6) | **Yes** | Two tips of one generation and height naming different blocks are evidence (`Client::Equivocation`); a node holding it (`data/equivocation.dat`) refuses that generation's tips above its height — commits and production stop, reads go on, the beacon takes only a certificate — until a later generation is certified. Down on `STATUS.equivocation`, up by `EQUIVOCATION_ADD`; relays and miners detect it from their upstreams' tips and push it up ([B018](DECISIONS.md#b018--equivocation-evidence-by-block-hash-halt-at-the-tip-gate-resume-on-a-later-generation)) — normative in [BEACON_AUTHORITY.md § Equivocation halt](../../docs/contracts/BEACON_AUTHORITY.md#equivocation-halt) |
| Recovery action (ba7) | **Yes** | The recovery set signs `T_RECOVERY` naming a new admin set; pending in the authority (so checkpoints carry it), it freezes the admin set (no `T_CONFIG`, no certificate, also out of band) and replaces it in place at the first block of its epoch, `delayEpochs` later. Shown on `STATUS.recovery`, `pp-client status`, error-level alerts each epoch. A genesis renewal must carry the admin set in force ([B019](DECISIONS.md#b019--recovery-a-record-of-its-own-pending-in-the-authority-applied-in-place-at-its-epoch)) — normative in [BEACON_AUTHORITY.md § Recovery](../../docs/contracts/BEACON_AUTHORITY.md#recovery--t_recovery-record-19) |
